## Outstanding licensed manufacturer images

- **Tesla Wall Connector:** current EU product photography permission remains unresolved. [Official product images](https://shop.tesla.com/nl_nl/product/wall-connector) are linked; publicly accessible does not mean unrestricted reproduction. Older Australian/US Commons examples were researched and rejected as lead imagery for the current EU configuration.
- **EcoFlow PowerOcean:** [official product page](https://energy.ecoflow.com/eu/products/powerocean) and documentation centre researched. No blanket reproduction permission established for the product photos. Architectural visual is used as a labelled temporary substitute; the page links to genuine manufacturer product imagery.
- **GoodWe:** [Lynx F](https://en.goodwe.com/lynx-f) and [publicity materials](https://emea.goodwe.com/publicity-material) researched. Public brochure access alone was not treated as a general image licence. Exact inverter/Lynx product photography still needs cleared use; system diagrams are original explanatory HTML/CSS.
- **Samsung:** current [Samsung-authored Ambrava R290 brochure](https://ambrava.nl/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Samsung-EHS-Mono-R290-Leaflet-Ambrava-web.pdf) used for technical verification. The Dropbox folder `/!allroundklusjesman/warmtepomp brochures/Samsung` contains 2023 HT Quiet, TDM Plus and ClimateHub PDFs; these were not republished or used to depict the R290 range. Current EHS Mono R290 product-photo permission remains open.
- **Whspr:** [manufacturer](https://whspr.net/) and [installer information](https://whspr.net/installateurs/) researched. A press/media contact is available, but no general reuse permission was established. Authentic product-photo permission remains open. No request was sent to the manufacturer on the owner's behalf.

These missing rights are not hidden by fabricated branded equipment. Replace the corresponding architectural substitutes with cleared original photos when available; retain the labels for any remaining AI scenes.
